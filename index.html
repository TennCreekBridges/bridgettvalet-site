About

I'm a creative director who builds. I joined Jackson Kayak in April 2013 and now run creative, brand and ecommerce across Jackson, Werner, Eddyline and KIALOA, from the first sketch to the checkout page.

I grew up camping on the Juniata River in Pennsylvania, and I try to put that same joy and wonder into every blade I design. I like being close to the product: choosing colors, testing prototypes on the water and turning what I learn into graphics, pages and launch campaigns that sell it.
